# Figure sources and reuse

Both figures are reproduced from the published article:

> Islam, J.; Datta, D.; Akhter, F. Trustworthy AI-Powered Intrusion Detection for the Internet of Medical Things (IoMT): A Review. *Sensors* 2026, 26, 5182. https://doi.org/10.3390/s26165182

© 2026 by the authors. Licensee MDPI, Basel, Switzerland. Published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence ([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)). The figures were extracted unchanged from the embedded images in the published PDF and composited onto a white background. They have not been modified in any other way.

| File | Article figure | Caption (as published, abridged) |
|---|---|---|
| `fig1_prisma_flow.png` | Figure 1 | PRISMA-guided study selection process illustrating identification, duplicate removal, language filtering, title screening, abstract screening, full-text eligibility assessment, quality assessment, and final study inclusion. |
| `fig5_trustworthy_ai_framework.png` | Figure 5 | Integrated framework for trustworthy AI-powered IoMT intrusion detection. Detection performance forms the technical foundation, while explainability enables transparency, stakeholder trust, human oversight, regulatory compliance, and deployment-ready Healthcare 5.0 cybersecurity. |

Figure 1 shows the PRISMA counts **as reported**. Which of those counts can and cannot be traced to original records is set out in [`../methodology/STUDY_SELECTION.md`](../methodology/STUDY_SELECTION.md).

Not included:
- Figures 4 and 7 (qualitative radar-style comparisons), because no underlying data exist.
- Figures that would duplicate article content without adding to this repository.
- Any third-party or publisher graphics.
